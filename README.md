# Career OS

A personalized career management system designed to help you track, optimize, and accelerate your professional growth.

## 🚀 Overview

Career OS is your all-in-one command center for career development. It combines structured workflows, intelligent agents, and data tracking to help you:

- Define and track career targets
- Manage job applications systematically
- Prepare for interviews with AI coaching
- Build and maintain professional relationships
- Research companies and roles intelligently
- Document your achievements and proof of work

## 🎯 Core Components

### 1. **Profile & Proof**
- `PROFILE.md`: Your professional identity, skills, and value proposition
- `PROOF.md`: Concrete evidence of your capabilities (projects, achievements, metrics)

### 2. **Target Definition**
- `TARGETS.md`: Clear, actionable career goals with timelines and success criteria

### 3. **Intelligent Agents**
Five specialized agents work together to manage your career:

| Agent | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Manager** | Orchestrates your career strategy and coordinates other agents |
| **Researcher** | Gathers intelligence on companies, roles, and market trends |
| **Outreach** | Manages networking, introductions, and relationship building |
| **Application** | Handles job applications, resumes, and cover letters |
| **Interview Coach** | Prepares you for interviews with mock sessions and feedback |

### 4. **Data Tracking**
CSV files track all aspects of your career journey:
- Companies you're researching or targeting
- Jobs you've applied to or are considering
- People in your professional network
- Outreach activities and follow-ups
- Study materials and skill development

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Clone this repository**
   ```bash
   git clone git@github.com:naet001/career-os-template.git
   cd career-os-template
   ```

2. **Customize your profile**
   - Edit `careeros/PROFILE.md` with your information
   - Populate `careeros/PROOF.md` with your achievements

3. **Define your targets**
   - Review and update `careeros/TARGETS.md`

4. **Set up your data**
   - Populate the CSV files in `careeros/data/` with your existing information

5. **Launch your agents**
   - Configure each agent in the `agents/` directory
   - Start with the Manager agent to coordinate your strategy
